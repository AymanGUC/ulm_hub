🎯 Eligibility & Topic Assignment

Before your selection can be officially confirmed, the university reviews your academic records. Your GPA must not be above 3.0 . You must also provide an updated CV along with a targeted motivation letter detailing why you want to join the track and focus on your selected topic.
Students must first select and rank their preferred choices directly on the system. Allocation runs on a first-come, first-served basis linked with meeting the 3.0 GPA cutoff and finishing the down payment. Usually, only 1 student is assigned to an individual topic, unless special capacity allows a professor to split a topic.

💶 Tuition Fees & Financial Schedule

Students pay the exact equivalent of their regular Cairo semester tuition fees , converted into Euros. While standard academic excellence scholarships apply to your regular semester tuition balance, local financial aid packages do not apply to this international track.
The structured payment order from the Finance Department follows this official timeline:
Installment Step Amount Due Deadline
Initial Down Payment 500 Euro (Non-Refundable) Upon system topic confirmation
Rest of Down Payment 800 Euro 9 December 2026
2nd Installment (Total Tuition minus 1300) Euro 5 March 2027
No blocked bank account is required . Because your stay in Germany is structured as a short, focused research window of only two to three months, you are exempt from this protocol. Visa procedures are officially facilitated by the university.

🤝 Thesis Supervision Journey & Process Expectations

Your graduation thesis follows a highly structured milestone timeline designed to support your development across both continents:
  • Phase 1 (Egypt - First 2 Months): You work closely with your internal GUC supervisor to build a comprehensive preliminary Thesis Outline and Project Proposal. Once confirmed or updated by your partner research group in Germany, you start your literature review, setup datasets, and build your baseline framework, algorithms, or code blocks.
  • Phase 2 (Germany - Next 2 Months): You travel to Germany to focus heavily on the core development task, experimentation phase, and evaluation testing under the on-site supervision and mentorship of your Baden-Württemberg professor.
Yes. Your GUC supervisor participates actively throughout the entire process to guarantee continuous academic alignment. While you are working on your development tasks in Germany, you will maintain consistent progress updates and draft review loops shared transparently between both supervisors.
While in Germany, you will arrange recurring office hour sessions to meet with your host research group on a weekly or bi-weekly basis. To ensure proper milestone documentation and professional engineering tracking, it is highly expected that Minutes of Meetings (MoM) are formally recorded for these tracking loops.
Upon completing your final draft iterations, you will formally present and defend your research project. Defenses can take place on-site at the GUC Cairo campus or directly at your host's campus in Germany. The non-traveling co-supervision panel from the other partner institution will join the presentation synchronously online.

📝 Step-by-Step Application Protocol

  1. Check the available system options, evaluate research specifics, and select your preferred thesis topics.
  2. Fill in the application form completely.
  3. Obtain signatures from your primary GUC supervisor and Faculty Dean.
  4. Submit your updated CV, motivation letter, and the signed form directly to Student Affairs.
  5. Collect your explicit Payment Order from the Finance Department to deposit your initial down payment.
