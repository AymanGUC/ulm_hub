BSc Thesis Co-Supervision Process Guide

Operational Framework and Joint Evaluation Policies for Academic Supervisors across Egypt and Germany hubs.

📢

Language Policy Directive

The official language of instruction, supervision communication, milestone reporting, written manuscript drafting, and final defense panels is strictly English across all partner tracks.

🗓️ 4-Month Sequential Execution Timeline (Spring 2027)

Month 1 (February)

Proposal & Outlines

Students draft the preliminary Thesis Outline & Project Proposal in Cairo under GUC supervisor direction.

Month 2 (March)

Baseline Preparation

German co-supervisors confirm or refine outlines. Students begin data prep, literature reviews, and initial coding in Egypt.

Months 3-4 (April–May)

Germany Execution Stay

Students live in Germany, accessing physical campus workspaces. Heavy focus on execution, algorithm testing, and draft iterations.

Evaluation Phase

Defense & Grading

Joint evaluation via standardized grading spreadsheets. International defenses run synchronously in-person and online.

🔄 Structural Mechanics & Supervision Roles

The student maintains a dual-guided milestone path. During the first 2 months in Cairo (February & March), principal tracking is driven by the internal GUC supervisor. During the following 2 months in Germany (April & May), the focus shifts to hands-on development and experimentation under the direct on-site guidance of the Baden-Württemberg partner professor. GUC teaching staff will also travel concurrently to Germany to ensure cross-institutional alignment.
While operating in Cairo, the student must produce a comprehensive preliminary Thesis Outline and formal project proposal. This dossier must explicitly define:
  • The target Research Gap
  • The central Research Question
  • The Proposed Research Method
  • A clear Project Plan & Timeline
This file is sent to the German research group for confirmation or refinement before baseline engineering begins.
To ensure minimal overhead for host universities, students integrate directly into the host group's pre-existing laboratory environment. Students agree on structured office hour sessions with their host co-supervisors, visiting the research group on a weekly or bi-weekly cadence for feedback and evaluation updates. This task can be entirely delegated by the German professor to a post-doc or senior PhD researcher within their institute.

📊 Grading Metrics & Defense Protocols

Grading is structured as a collaborative, fully transparent partnership. At the conclusion of the thesis window, a standardized Excel sheet populated with clear grading metrics is exchanged between institutions. Both sides review each other's score records for reference, and the final graduation grade is computed as the exact mathematical average of both supervisors' independent grades.
Defenses are organized as international events to showcase research output to German conglomerates and regional industry partners. The final presentation can take place on-site at the GUC Cairo campus or directly at the host German university campus (depending on professor preference). The non-traveling supervisor panel joins the evaluation committee synchronously online. All deliverables and panel arguments must be rendered in English.
